Click a mode to observe charge behavior.

Free Electron

Negative charge carriers. In N-type Silicon, Phosphorus adds these "extra" electrons that move freely through the conduction band.

Hole

Positive charge carriers. In P-type Silicon, Boron creates a "vacancy." A hole moves when a valence electron hops into this empty spot.

~~~
Photon

Particles of light. Emitted when an electron "falls" into a hole. This drop in energy level releases a wave of electromagnetic radiation.
